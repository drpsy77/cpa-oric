Welcome to CP/A!
"A>" prompts you for a command.
ESC completes a command you started;
on an empty line, ESC lists them
all: built-in, .COM and .BAT.
FUNCT opens menus, HELP gives help.

Writing:
 EDIT NOTES.TXT   write a text
                  (CTRL-S saves,
                  CTRL-Q quits)
 TYPE NOTES.TXT   read it again
 DIR              your files
