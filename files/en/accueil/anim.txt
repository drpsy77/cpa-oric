Welcome to CP/A!
"A>" prompts you for a command.
ESC completes a command you started;
on an empty line, ESC lists them
all: built-in, .COM and .BAT.
FUNCT opens menus, HELP gives help.

Pictures and animations:
 DEMO             all of it in turn
 VOIR CUBE.ANI    an animation
                  (a key: stop)
 VOIR LOGO.HIZ    a picture
 GRAPHER ANIMS    animations in a
                  program
 EDIT DEMO.BAT    the DEMO script
DIR shows the other files.
